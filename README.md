# Retail-Sales-Dashboard

## 1. Project Title / Headline

Retail Sales Performance Dashboard | Power BI Analytics & Business Intelligence Report

## 2. Short Description / Purpose

This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of retail business performance by analyzing sales, profit, orders, customers, and profit margins. The report helps stakeholders track key business metrics, identify trends, evaluate performance across regions and product categories, and make data-driven decisions to improve business growth and profitability.

## 4. Data Source

The dashboard is built using retail sales transaction data containing:

Sales Revenue
Profit
Profit Margin
Customer Information
Order Details
Product Categories
Regional/Location Data
Transaction Records

The data was cleaned, transformed, and modeled using Power Query and Power BI before creating interactive visualizations.

## 5. Features / Highlights
📊 Business Problem

Retail businesses generate large volumes of sales data, making it challenging to monitor performance and identify growth opportunities. This dashboard provides a centralized view of critical business metrics for faster analysis and decision-making.

🎯 Dashboard Objectives
Monitor overall sales and profitability.
Track customer and order performance.
Analyze profit margins across products and regions.
Identify top-performing categories and locations.
Support strategic business decisions with interactive reporting.
📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
Total Sales
Total Profit
Total Orders
Total Customers
Profit Margin %
🔍 Dashboard Features
Executive Summary View
Interactive KPI Cards
Sales Trend Analysis
Profit and Profit Margin Tracking
Customer Performance Insights
Order Analysis Dashboard
Dynamic Filters and Slicers
Cross-Filtering and Drill-Down Functionality
💡 Key Insights Generated
Identifies high-revenue and high-profit product categories.
Highlights top-performing customer segments.
Tracks order volume and sales trends over time.
Evaluates regional sales performance.
Monitors profitability and business growth metrics.
🚀 Business Impact
Enables faster and more informed decision-making.
Reduces manual reporting efforts through automation.
Improves visibility into sales and profitability.
Helps identify opportunities for revenue growth and cost optimization.
